## The Problem

- Clinicians relied on REE estimates from generalized formulas based on age, height, weight, and activity level
- Those formulas could miss a patient's actual measured REE by a wide margin, in one real reading, by 18%
- No individualized way to account for a patient's own metabolic variation
- Clinical workflows left little room for setup, testing, and review to be anything but fast and reliable
- HIPAA and FDA compliance requirements applied to every screen handling patient data

<Callout eyebrow="Problem to solve" center>
  <p>The app had to be invisible when it worked, and unmistakably clear the moment it didn't.</p>
</Callout>

## Strategy & Execution

Constraints shaped every decision: HIPAA and FDA compliance, reliable BLE communication to the device, measurement logic that mirrored the hardware's behavior exactly, and local-only storage for the partners who required it. The guiding principle was clarity under pressure: streamlined flows, clear system feedback, minimal cognitive load, explicit state handling, and interfaces built for speed.
