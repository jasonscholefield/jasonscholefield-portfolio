## Strategy & Execution

The redesign shifted ownership to CRAs and PIs while preserving compliance. The new data change workflow:

1. CRA selects the data point requiring change
2. CRA selects a reason and digitally signs the request
3. PI reviews, approves or declines, and signs off

<ContentSplit heading="Wireframing the New Ownership Model" image={wireframeImg} imageAlt="Wireframes for the new data change workflow, mapping point selection, reason and justification, and PI review and sign-off" imagePosition="left">
  <p>Before touching the live product, I wireframed the full data change flow: point selection, reason and justification, and PI review and sign-off, mapping how ownership moves from CRA to PI at each step.</p>
</ContentSplit>

## The Agentic Dashboard

Beyond this workflow redesign, I worked with Medable's design team to build out every screen in the platform's broader agentic AI experience. Much of it came together during an on-site workshop in Chicago, where most of the company spent a week designing and building the experience on the fly to pitch a major sponsor in the industry.

The platform itself is a model-agnostic system built for GxP-regulated environments, connecting to 15+ clinical and enterprise tools like Veeva, Oracle, Medidata, and Snowflake through more than 30 validated connectors. Every recommendation an agent makes stays fully observable, explainable, and auditable, which matters as much as the recommendation itself in a regulated trial.
