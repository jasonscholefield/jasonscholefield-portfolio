## The Problem

Paid media landing pages (PMLPs) are critical to UTI's enrollment funnel. A redesign aimed to align the PMLP with a broader website refresh while supporting new business priorities: visual consistency updates, elevated Auto and Diesel programs, and additional content components.

## Strategy & Execution

The initial redesign (v2) underperformed despite visual improvements. Analysis revealed the real issues: conversion actions were positioned lower on the page, users faced information overload, visual hierarchy prioritized exploration over decision-making, and elevating multiple programs reduced perceived choice clarity. The team had, in effect, optimized for brand alignment and content richness at the expense of speed and intent.

> Optimizing for brand alignment and content richness came at the expense of speed and intent.

## The Pivot

Rather than iterating visually again, the team refocused on user behavior and funnel intent, guided by three principles:

- High-intent users prioritize clarity and speed over exploration
- Discoverability matters less than persuasion at this stage of the funnel
- Proven patterns warrant protection without strong evidence to replace them

Key changes: restoring immediate CTA access, simplifying content density, reducing program emphasis, and designing the page to be scanned, not read.

## Outcome & Learnings

- Visual alignment doesn't guarantee performance
- Paid media pages serve intent, not curiosity
- Elevating more options can reduce clarity instead of adding value
- Iteration requires humility and rapid feedback loops
