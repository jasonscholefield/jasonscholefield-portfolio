## Strategy & Execution

Design priorities balanced multiple user types, real-time availability, and integrations with the property management systems communities already used, like Yardi, ResMan, and Entrata. That meant building toward three related but distinct offerings instead of a single flow: short-term parking for residents and their guests, long-term rentable items like storage and additional spaces, and parking enforcement so communities can identify unauthorized vehicles at any hour, no on-site patrol required.

Across all three, the same priorities held:

- Clear discovery mechanisms for available spaces
- Streamlined reservation workflows for both short- and long-term parking
- Transparent status communication for residents and guests
- Manager monitoring tools and unified operational dashboards
