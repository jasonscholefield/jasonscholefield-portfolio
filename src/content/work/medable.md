## The Problem

Medable's decentralized clinical trial platform serves CRAs, Principal Investigators, patients, and sponsors in a highly regulated environment. An end-to-end journey map surfaced the real issue: ownership gaps between roles, manual handoffs creating delays and risk, and a support team functioning as a permanent workaround for product limitations.

The existing workflow was operationally expensive — CRAs submitted change requests to support, developers manually updated database records, turnaround was slow and error-prone, and Medable absorbed unnecessary cost for routine work.

## Strategy & Execution

> Because Medable operates in a regulated environment, fixing these issues required precision and restraint rather than sweeping redesigns.

The redesign shifted ownership to CRAs and PIs while preserving compliance. The new data change workflow:

1. CRA selects the data point requiring change
2. CRA selects a reason and digitally signs the request
3. PI reviews, approves or declines, and signs off

## Outcome

- Eliminated Medable support involvement in routine updates
- Reduced correction turnaround time
- Strengthened accountability between CRAs and PIs
- Lowered operational overhead
- Reinforced auditability and trust in the platform
