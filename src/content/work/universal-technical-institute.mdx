## Strategy & Execution

Rather than iterate on the visual design again, I turned to behavioral data to understand exactly where high-intent users were dropping off. I found v2 had quietly optimized for the wrong thing entirely.

<ContentSplit heading="Grounded in the Competitive Landscape" image={c6Img} imageAlt="Research conducted between the second and third versions" imagePosition="left">
  <p>I started the pivot from v2 to v3 with another round of research, this time focused specifically on how competitors handled conversion-critical moments, not general navigation patterns.</p>
</ContentSplit>

## The Pivot

Reversing course meant making the case to leadership that v2's visual consistency with the broader site refresh wasn't worth what it was costing in conversions. With that alignment in place, I refocused the page on user behavior and funnel intent instead, guided by three principles:

- High-intent users prioritize clarity and speed over exploration
- Discoverability matters less than persuasion at this stage of the funnel
- Proven patterns warrant protection without strong evidence to replace them

I restored immediate CTA access, simplified content density, reduced program emphasis, and designed the page to be scanned, not read.
