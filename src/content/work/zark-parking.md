## The Problem

Parking is a recurring pain point that impacts reputation, retention, and operations for multifamily properties. Residents struggled to locate available spaces, property teams relied on manual processes, unutilized spaces represented lost revenue, and enforcement consumed staff resources.

## Strategy & Execution

Design priorities balanced multiple user types, real-time availability, and integrations with existing property management systems:

- Clear discovery mechanisms for available spaces
- Streamlined reservation workflows for both short- and long-term parking
- Transparent status communication for residents
- Manager monitoring tools and unified operational dashboards

## Outcome

- Reduced dependency on manual coordination
- Improved resident parking access experience
- Monetized previously underutilized spaces
- Improved compliance and usage visibility for property teams

## Learnings

> Marketplace-style systems in niche domains require a careful balance between supply, demand, and incentives.

Operational tools need to appear simple while managing real complexity underneath — and meaningful product impact often comes from clarifying existing infrastructure rather than inventing something entirely new.
