## The Problem

This project served two goals at once: visualizing a game concept developed over several years, and gaining real Webflow proficiency through execution rather than tutorials. The focus was on translating the concept beyond static mockups — exploring how presentation, motion, and structure could bring a concept to life.

## The Concept

The hypothetical game combines large-scale, squad-based multiplayer gameplay with a faction-driven, darker sci-fi setting. Rather than designing a complete game, the project aimed to establish tone, worldbuilding, and suggested gameplay roles — and to create a believable landing-page experience around that speculative framing.

## Strategy & Execution

The entire build happened directly in Webflow, with emphasis on page structure and scroll pacing, visual hierarchy across long-form layouts, atmospheric imagery, and section transitions that guide narrative flow. Trade-offs were made deliberately: an image-heavy build with accepted load times, interactions chosen to favor exploration over efficiency, and accessibility/responsiveness left short of production-hardened.

## Outcome & Learnings

Webflow rewards strong structural thinking. Long-form pages depend critically on hierarchy and pacing, concept work benefits from real constraints, and end-to-end builds expose gaps that mockups conceal. Working within a problem space you're motivated by makes tool mastery far more achievable.
