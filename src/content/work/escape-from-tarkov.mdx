## Overview

Escape from Tarkov is a deeply complex extraction shooter built around inventory management, weapon customization, economy systems, and high-consequence gameplay. This exploration looked at how clearer UX patterns and information hierarchy could improve usability while preserving the game's hardcore identity.

## The Problem

The game's depth creates real friction: heavy memorization demands, reliance on external wikis, trial-and-error learning, and dense menu navigation. The opportunity was in improving how information surfaces, not in oversimplifying the underlying systems.

## Strategy & Execution

**Ammunition systems:** Critical stats were often hidden or hard to compare quickly. Explorations focused on clearer stat hierarchy, better comparison visibility, more readable penetration and damage information, and faster decision-making during loadout prep, with the goal of reducing dependence on third-party spreadsheets.

**Scopes and optics:** A lightweight preview system let players inspect reticles and compare optics before committing to a purchase, reducing experimentation friction.

## Learnings

> Hardcore systems still benefit from clarity and hierarchy.

Expert users prioritize efficiency over simplification, information density requires disciplined prioritization, and strong UX can preserve complexity while still reducing friction.
