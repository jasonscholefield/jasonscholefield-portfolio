## The Problem

Breezing is a medical device that measures a patient's Resting Energy Expenditure through breath analysis. Breezing Pro is its HIPAA-compliant companion app, connecting via Bluetooth to initiate tests and securely store clinical results. Clinicians previously relied on REE estimates from generalized formulas based on age, height, weight, and activity level — imprecise, and blind to individual metabolic variation. The app had to fit into clinical workflows defined by extreme time constraints, where setup, testing, and review all needed to be fast and reliable.

## Strategy & Execution

Constraints shaped every decision: HIPAA and FDA compliance, reliable BLE communication, measurement logic that mirrored device behavior exactly, and — for some partners — local-only storage. The guiding principle was clarity under pressure: streamlined flows, clear system feedback, minimal cognitive load, explicit state handling, and interfaces built for speed.

## Outcome

- Secure BLE pairing and device communication
- Test initiation and monitoring
- Compliant results viewing and storage
- A parallel local-only storage version for partners who required it
- Over-the-air firmware updates

## Learnings

> Medical accuracy means little without usable delivery.

Designing for clinicians requires ruthless prioritization, hardware-integrated UX must account for failure states, and compliance and usability aren't mutually exclusive.
