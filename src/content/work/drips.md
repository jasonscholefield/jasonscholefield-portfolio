## The Problem

Drips provides conversational SMS marketing solutions for healthcare and enterprise clients. Before this project, clients requested statistics directly from Customer Success Managers, who manually pulled data from legacy systems into spreadsheets and emailed screenshots weekly. It created unnecessary overhead, prevented clients from exploring their own data, and scaled poorly.

## Strategy & Execution

Research centered on why data was needed, what decisions clients made with it, who interpreted it, and where the friction actually lived. The finding: the dashboard was never about conversion or growth actions — it was about clarity, trust, and self-service.

Rather than designing an exhaustive platform, the approach focused on foundational usability:

- Make important metrics immediately visible
- Reduce cognitive load through hierarchy and flow
- Clearly communicate filtered data views
- Design for iteration rather than completeness

## Outcome

- Enabled on-demand performance data access for clients
- Reduced Customer Success reliance for routine reporting
- Improved transparency and trust in the underlying metrics
- Created a scalable foundation for future analytics features
- Shifted the product from a service-mediated to a self-serve model

## Learnings

> Dashboards should reflect user questions, not data availability.

Self-service requires strong defaults and clear context, and a focused v1 release outperforms waiting for completeness.
